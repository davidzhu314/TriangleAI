## Issues

1. **The scripted Compare beat sets up a 0 plan score** (to Aroovee). DEMO.md says: move risk to maximum, then switch to the right radial route. At those weights the right radial route ranks last, so regret = Rmax and the plan score is 0. The Results beat then leads with a penalty and "Revise the plan", which is the opposite of the story. Options: reset the weights (or move the slider back) before switching; or switch first and move the slider after, to show the re-rank only; or drive the route that ranks 1 at the final weights (left femoral, which needs rotation at the arch).
2. **The Compare weights and table are below the fold at 1440 × 900** (to Aroovee). The sliders start at about y = 920. The presenter has to scroll, and then the route overlay is off screen while the ranking changes. The table's last column ("Estimated fluoro time") is also clipped at that width.
3. **Route rows and switch buttons don't name the entry site** (to Aroovee). They say "Shortest, gentlest, least radiation" and "Switch to Shortest, gentlest, least radiation (Soft angled)", while the script says "the right radial route". The presenter has to know the mapping.

Either stop logging at `target_reached`, or auto-finish. At least, the checklist now tells the presenter to release Space and click Finish drive.
